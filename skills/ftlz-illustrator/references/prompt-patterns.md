# Prompt Patterns

Choose the pattern that best matches the user's article or requested visual. Mix patterns only when necessary.

## Cover

Best for article headers and concept-first visuals.

- STRUCTURE: one central metaphor object or scene, character placed as guide/operator.
- NODES: title, one core object, 1-3 supporting icons.
- RELATIONSHIPS: character interacts with or points to the core metaphor.
- Text density: low.

## Flow

Best for workflows, tutorials, tools, and processes.

- STRUCTURE: left-to-right or top-to-bottom sequence with 3-5 steps.
- NODES: each step has one icon and one short label.
- RELATIONSHIPS: arrows connect the steps; character explains or operates one key step.
- Text density: medium.

Example node labels: "读文章", "抓重点", "定构图", "写提示词".

## Comparison

Best for two versions, two interpretations, before/after, or tradeoffs.

- STRUCTURE: left and right panels with a clear center divider or bridge.
- NODES: left item, right item, central question mark, scale, fork, or character.
- RELATIONSHIPS: character compares, connects, or looks puzzled between the two sides.
- Text density: medium.

## Explainer

Best for abstract ideas, technical concepts, historical clues, and conceptual models.

- STRUCTURE: central metaphor with surrounding labels.
- NODES: main metaphor, 2-4 concept nodes, one character action.
- RELATIONSHIPS: arrows, dotted lines, callouts, or magnifier links show how the concept works.
- Text density: medium.

## Path Or Location

Best for maps, directions, origins, citations, transformations, and route-like arguments.

- STRUCTURE: simplified route, map-like path, or node chain on a white background.
- NODES: places, sources, milestones, or textual clues as short labels.
- RELATIONSHIPS: arrows show direction, branch, reversal, or uncertainty.
- Text density: medium; keep labels short.

## Scene

Best for narrative or emotional article passages.

- STRUCTURE: one clear mini-scene with character, object, and action.
- NODES: character, one environment hint, one symbolic object.
- RELATIONSHIPS: character's posture and expression carry the meaning.
- Text density: low.

## Standard Style Case

This is a reusable pattern style, not fixed content:

```text
STRUCTURE: 中央是一台原创小发明机器，左侧输入材料，右侧输出结果。
NODES: 左侧输入卡片；中央机器；右侧输出卡片；下方四个步骤图标。
RELATIONSHIPS: 角色站在机器旁，把输入材料放入入口；机器另一端吐出结果；下方步骤用箭头串联。
STYLE: 复古圆润搞笑科幻漫画感，小发明机器，手绘箭头，Q版人物，明快色块，简洁留白。
ASPECT: 16:9
CONSTRAINTS: 中文标签必须简短清晰；保持角色一致；机器原创，不使用真实品牌商标；人物肢体和手部完整。
AVOID: direct copy of any existing manga character or machine, real logos, QR codes, long text blocks, cluttered background, bad anatomy, extra limbs, deformed hands, malformed fingers.
```
